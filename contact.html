Contact Xoft Tools

We read every message. The most common reasons people write in:

  • Request a tool — tell us which calculator or generator you wish existed. The best ideas get built first.
  • Report an error — if a calculation looks wrong or a page misbehaves on your device, send the page URL and a screenshot if you can.
  • Suggest an improvement — guides that could be clearer, tools that need a feature, anything.

Email us

Reach us at hello@websiteads.vercel.app. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days.

Before you write

Check the FAQ on the relevant tool page — common questions about how each tool works are answered there. For privacy questions, see our privacy policy. Xoft Tools does not offer financial, legal, or investment advice; calculator results are estimates for planning purposes.

What happens after you write

Every message is read by a person, not a bot. Tool requests go onto our public build list: the most-requested calculators and generators are built first, and the slate already published on this site grew exactly that way. If you reported an error, we reproduce the calculation by hand, fix the tool if it is wrong, and correct the guide if the wording caused the confusion. We do not send marketing emails, and contacting us never adds you to a mailing list.

What makes a good tool request

The requests that get built fastest include three things: the job you are trying to do ("I price freelance design work and need a rate that covers taxes"), the numbers you would type in, and one real example with the answer you expect. Screenshots of a calculator you like help too. Vague requests are still welcome — they just take longer to turn into a tool. Teachers, students, creators, freelancers, and small business owners are all equally welcome to write in.

Corrections and accuracy

Accuracy matters more to us than being right the first time. If a guide states a rate, threshold, or rule that has changed, tell us and we will update the page and note the correction. Our calculators use standard published formulas — the amortization formula for loans, compound interest for savings goals, gross margin for pricing — and each guide explains its formula in plain language so you can check the math yourself before relying on it.

Can you check my numbers privately?

You never need to send us your numbers at all. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your own browser: the figures you type are never transmitted to us, stored, or visible to anyone at Xoft Tools. That is by design — a budget or loan calculation is personal, and a free tool should not need your data to work. If you do include figures in an email, we use them only to answer your question and delete the thread afterwards.

Business, partnerships, and advertising

For partnership ideas, content collaborations, or questions about advertising on the site, use the same email address with "Business" in the subject line. We are a small independent site, so replies on business topics can take a little longer than the usual 2–3 business days. We do not sell guest posts, paid links, or placements inside calculator results — tool outputs are never influenced by advertisers.
