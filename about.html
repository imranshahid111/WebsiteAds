About {{BRAND}}

{{BRAND}} is a free collection of online tools and calculators built for three groups of people: creators who publish videos, posts, and content; students who need quick answers for essays, budgets, and study sessions; and small business owners who want practical numbers without hiring a consultant.

Why these tools exist

Too many everyday tasks still require a spreadsheet, a paid app, or a confusing form. Splitting a paycheck into a budget, checking what a loan really costs, writing a prompt for an AI video tool, counting the words in an essay, or making a password that cannot be guessed in seconds — these are small jobs that should take seconds, not minutes. Every tool here runs instantly in your browser, on any device, with no account and no paywall.

How the tools are built

All tools are hand-built with plain web technology and tested for accuracy. Calculations like the 50/30/20 budget split and the loan EMI formula use standard financial math. Generators like the password tool use your browser's cryptographically secure random number generator. Nothing runs on a server, which means your inputs never leave your device.

Who runs {{BRAND}}

{{BRAND}} is an independent project, maintained by a small team that builds and tests each tool before publishing. We add new tools based on what readers ask for — if there is a calculator or generator you wish existed, send the idea through the contact page.

Our content standards

Guides on this site are written in plain English and reviewed for accuracy before publishing. Budgeting and loan pages are educational only and are not financial advice — see our disclaimer for the full picture.
