Calculator-specific notes

Budget calculators. The 50/30/20 rule is a starting framework popularized by Elizabeth Warren, not a personal recommendation. Housing costs in high-cost cities routinely exceed 30% of income; our guide explains how to adapt the ratios rather than treat them as a pass/fail test.

Loan calculators. Our EMI calculations use the standard amortization formula and exclude lender-specific items such as origination fees, insurance, taxes, and prepayment penalties. Your lender's actual payment schedule is the authoritative one — compare our estimate against the lender's disclosure statement before signing anything.

Business calculators. Margin, markup, and freelance-rate results depend entirely on the costs and assumptions you enter. They do not include income tax obligations, which vary by country, state, and business structure; treat the outputs as planning estimates and confirm tax treatment with an accountant.
